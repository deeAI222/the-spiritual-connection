Frequently asked questions

**Summary:** How do time zones work?

Calendly shows times in your local clock and saves the appointment correctly for both of us (you and UK time).

**Summary:** How do I pay?

After you book, I’ll email payment details. Packages: £60/$80 single, £150/$200 for 3, £300/$400 for 6.

**Summary:** What video platform do you use?

Usually Zoom or Google Meet. You’ll receive a private link before the session.

**Summary:** Are sessions confidential?

Yes, within the normal ethical and legal boundaries of counselling practice.

**Summary:** Can I cancel or reschedule?

Please give 24 hours’ notice if you can. You can often reschedule from the Calendly confirmation email.

**Summary:** Do you work outside the UK?

Yes. Clients join from many countries. The calendar handles timezones automatically.
